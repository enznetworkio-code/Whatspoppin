privacy

Starter policy. Last updated Nov 2026. Have a lawyer review it before a public launch.

what we collect

Your email, username, optional name, bio, profile photo and color, the posts and comments you make (photos, captions, and a blurred location area if you choose to share it), who you follow and who follows you, messages you send, and your early-access signup details.

location

If you allow it, we save a blurred area (a rounded grid square, never your exact position) with a post. You choose how blurry in settings. You can turn it off for any post.

anonymous posts

Anonymous posts show a random name like anon.4821 to other people. They are not tied to your username in the public feed or on your profile. We keep a private record that the post is yours so you can delete it.

how long things last

Posts stop appearing in the app 24 hours after they are made. Expired posts may stay in our database until we clear them. You can delete your own posts and your whole account any time in settings.

who can see what

Signed-in members can see posts, public profiles, comments and public follow counts. Only the two people in a chat can read it. Your email is never shown to other members.

safety

You can block, mute and report people and posts. Reports are reviewed by us and may lead to content removal or account removal. Members must be 13 or older.

your choices

Edit your profile, change blur, mute or block people, and delete your account in settings. To ask about your data, message us on TikTok @whtspoppingg.
