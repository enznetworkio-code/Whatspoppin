1. what this covers

this policy explains what what’s poppin (“we”, “us”) collects, why, and the choices you have. it covers our early-access website and the early-access app built into it, and the iPhone app as it launches. by using them you agree to this policy.

2. what we collect

what you give us

  • account: your email address and password. passwords are handled by Google’s Firebase Authentication. we never see or store your password.
  • username and access code: the username you pick and a code we generate for you. we also record whether you said you’re under 18, so we can apply safeguards.
  • early-access details: your city, the interests you pick, any person you chose to follow before signing up, and which invite link you came from.
  • profile (optional): a display name, a short bio and a profile photo.
  • posts: the photo, an optional caption of up to 140 characters, and a time. if you turn on “show the blurred area”, a rounded location (see section 5).
  • activity: likes, who you follow and who follows you, circles you create or join, circle updates, reports you file, and messages you send.

what we collect automatically

  • a sign-in token stored in your browser so you stay logged in. you can remove it any time by logging out.
  • basic technical data such as IP address and device or browser type, which our hosting and database providers receive when your device connects to them.

3. how we use it

  • to run the service: create your account, show posts nearby, let people follow, message and join circles.
  • to keep people safe: review reports, remove content, limit or suspend accounts, and prevent abuse and spam.
  • to run early access: count signups, order the waitlist, and tell you when we launch in your city.
  • to improve the product and fix problems.
  • to meet legal obligations.

we do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

4. who can see what

  • everyone signed in can see posts (photo, caption, the name on the post, time, and the rounded area if you added one), like counts, and your public profile (username, display name, bio, photo).
  • only you can see your followers and following lists and your list of posts.
  • circle members can read updates posted in that circle. only the person who made the circle can post updates.
  • messages can only be read by the two people in the conversation, and only while you follow each other.
  • our team can access the database, including what is needed to handle reports and keep the service safe.

5. location and anonymous posts

location. we only ask for your location when you choose to show the area on a post. if you’ve already allowed location, nearby also uses it on your device to show how far a post is. your device’s exact position is rounded to a grid of roughly a third of a mile before anything is saved. we never store or show your exact spot. you can leave the location off on any post.

anonymous posts. when you post as anon, the post carries a random name like anon.4821 and nothing public connects it to your account. we keep a private link between your account and your own posts so you can see and delete them, and so we can act on reports. that means anonymous means anonymous to other people, not to us. we may use it to enforce our rules or respond to a valid legal request.

6. who we share it with

we use service providers to run what’s poppin and they handle data for us only to do that:

  • Google Firebase (authentication and database) stores your account and content.
  • content delivery services (for example cdnjs, jsDelivr and Google Fonts) load scripts and fonts onto the page, so they receive your IP address and browser details when you visit.

we may also share information if the law requires it, to respond to a valid legal request, to protect someone from serious harm, or to enforce our terms. if we are ever part of a merger or sale, your information may transfer as part of it and we would tell you first.

7. how long we keep it

  • posts disappear from the app 24 hours after you post. the underlying copy can remain in our database until we delete it.
  • account details stay until you delete your account. deletion in the app is immediate. small leftovers, such as likes or comments on other people’s posts, can remain until those posts expire, and encrypted backups can take a little longer to clear.
  • reports and related records are kept as long as we need them to keep people safe or meet legal duties.
  • messages, circle updates and likes stay until you or we delete them.

8. your choices

  • edit or remove your name, bio and photo from your profile any time.
  • delete any of your posts from your profile.
  • unfollow people, remove followers, or leave a circle.
  • log out to clear the sign-in token from your browser.
  • delete your account yourself: in the iPhone app, open your profile, tap the menu, then “delete account”, and enter your password. this permanently removes your posts, circle photos and updates, follows, blocks, messages you sent, profile, username and login. the username becomes available again. circles you own that still have other members stay open for them.
  • or ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it, or to send you a copy, by emailing [your contact email]. we may need to confirm it’s you first.

depending on where you live, you may have extra rights, such as access, correction, deletion and objection. email us and we’ll help.

9. children and teens

you must be at least 13 to use what’s poppin. we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. if we learn we have, we’ll delete it. if you think a child under 13 has an account, email us.

we ask if you are under 18 so we can protect you. everyone can only message people they follow and who follow them back. if you’re under 18, check with a parent or guardian before using the service.

10. security

we use trusted providers and access rules that limit who can read each kind of data. no online service is perfectly secure, so we can’t promise absolute security. use a strong, unique password and don’t share your code or QR with people you don’t trust.

11. changes and contact

we may update this policy as the product grows. when we make a meaningful change, we’ll update the date above and tell you in the app or by email. questions or requests: [your contact email].
